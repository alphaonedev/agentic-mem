Compute and models are commoditizing. Memory is the layer that isn't solved — and can't be faked.

Every serious AI deployment is moving from a single assistant to fleets of agents — clusters, swarms, and hives acting continuously on real systems. The one thing they all lack is a reliable place to remember.

As agents graduate from demos to production, and into regulated, mission-critical work, the buyer's question changes. It stops being "how smart is the agent?" and becomes "can I trust what it remembers — and prove it?"

AgenticMem sells that trust.

The offering

What AgenticMem sells

Five ways to buy trust, scale, and peace of mind on top of the free substrate.

01

Managed Cloud

Hosted, multi-tenant, SLA-backed endpoint memory. Usage-based. The fastest path from "we have agents" to "our agents remember, safely."

02

Enterprise Federation

The certified configuration, productized — encrypted, attested, governed. On-prem, air-gapped, or in-VPC. PostgreSQL + AGE + pgvector.

03

Compliance & Certification

SOC 2, HIPAA, FedRAMP / StateRAMP, government accreditation — the moat that unlocks the mission-critical buyer.

04

Reflection-AI Curation

Managed cross-model memory curation — dedup, consolidation, and graph enrichment so agents never sit in a single model's echo chamber.

05

Support & Services

24/7 support, SLAs, migration, and reference architectures for agent fleets at scale.
